## Safety
The software intentionally does not invent official dates, syllabus versions, post codes, pattern, weightage, legal provisions, current affairs, or PYQ years. Where the exact current source is not locked, the UI displays SOURCE LOCK REQUIRED instead of a false official syllabus.
